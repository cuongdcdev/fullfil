# Red-team review: escrow and verifiers

Negative-path review of the escrow contract (`nearfullfil.near`) and the `email-verifier` and `cryptorefills-verifier` OutLayer workers, done on 2026-11-08 before the W9.8 mainnet upgrade. Line numbers refer to the reviewed commits named below; later commits moved code. The `in_person` kind (W10) and `token-verifier` (W11) were added after this review and are not covered by this matrix. They are covered, together with a re-check of this matrix at current HEAD, in [REDTEAM_W11.md](REDTEAM_W11.md). Results of the live mainnet attacks are in [MAINNET_ATTACK_TESTS.md](MAINNET_ATTACK_TESTS.md).

## 1. Scope and assumptions

Reviewed the escrow source at commit `5fabc8c` and verifier worker source at commit `6d91f2f` on 2026-11-08; these reviewed files were unchanged in later concurrent HEAD `7b9367b`. This is a defensive review of our escrow, both verifier workers, and the read-only proof-blob/backend boundary. No contract, worker, or backend logic was changed by this task. The mainnet contract `nearfullfil.near` is assumed to run the verified escrow wasm (`ca2fff3c…`). The live configuration at review time: owner and arbiter are `nearfullfil.near`; relayer is `ab35c2cc…2bf5b27`; accepted/bond token is the single USDC NEP-141 ID; `min_bond=0`, `bond_per_job_bps=0`, `challenge_window_ns=0`, `min_order_amount=100000` (0.1 USDC), `max_orders=max_profiles=1000`. `cryptorefills_topup` is open, `cryptorefills_code` allows only `shopper.nearfullfil.near`, and `bank_transfer` requires approval. Top-up/code claim timeout is 15 minutes, proof timeout is 30 minutes, quote exclusivity is 2 minutes, and maximum deadline is 30 minutes. The relayer and proof-blob host are treated as potentially malicious; raw signed intents can bypass our backend.

**Status meaning:** `DEFENDED-tested` has a passing automated test (including pre-existing tests); `DEFENDED-untested` has a code guard but no direct test; `VULNERABLE` has a known secure-behavior gap (an ignored regression test is named where feasible); `ACCEPTED-RISK` is a deliberate economic or operational trust assumption. An ignored `vuln_` test asserts the desired secure behavior and fails on current code. File/line references identify the present guard or, for a gap, where the check is missing. A worker's `ok=false` is followed by on-chain `proof_rejected`, not a contract panic. The `auth_call` promise is detached by intents, so a rejected escrow receipt can coexist with a successful outer `execute_intents` transaction.

## 2. Findings

- **R-12 — arbitrary quote pin (fixed).** Funding now rejects a buyer quote, an unregistered account, or an account that fails the kind's access rule. The official shopper must register and remain eligible before the backend names it in `quoted_by`. A registered eligible colluder can still receive the two-minute exclusive window; buyer cancellation remains available.
- **R-21 — proof after order deadline (fixed).** Proof at or after the deadline fails; claim and proof windows are clamped to it. Funded and Claimed refund after deadline, unlocking a Claimed bond without slash. Sent and ProofRejected cannot expire; `timeout` at the proof-window end or deadline moves them to UnderReview. A proof submitted before deadline may still pay through its Verifying callback afterward. If Verifying times out at or after deadline, `timeout` moves it to UnderReview and a later callback is ignored. UnderReview retains buyer funds, the open-order slot, and the fulfiller's locked bond until an arbiter verdict; Verified remains payable through finalize or dispute.
- **R-25 — escrow-funded storage (mitigated).** Registration, funding, bonds, claims, ratings, recounts, and owner storage changes require a configurable free-NEAR reserve (default 1 NEAR, plus a 2,000-byte flush allowance). Existing `Stats.open_as_buyer` caps open orders at five per buyer, including migrated live orders. Sent, proof, dispute, and settlement paths for funded orders are exempt from the storage reserve so they can progress; proof still requires its OutLayer deposit and verification float. Sybil accounts can each open five orders; global caps remain the hard bound, and callers still pay no storage deposit or metered storage fee.
- **R-34 — rating of unpaid buyer verdict (fixed).** Star ratings now require `paid_at` to be set. Unpaid buyer resolutions cannot be rated; paid orders resolved later retain their rating path.
- **R-42 — ambiguous phone normalization (medium).** Fixed (W9.8): the worker and `packages/core` require strict E.164 using shared `beneficiary-vectors.json` tests; Cryptorefills must echo the shopper's E.164 value or verification fails closed.
- **R-43 — future completion timestamp (low).** Fixed (W9.8): `created_at` must be at most the worker's own clock plus five minutes, and verification fails closed when that clock is unavailable.
- **R-44 — signed bank note prefix collision (high).** Fixed (W9.8): matching requires a delimited order-ID token, tolerates whitespace inside it, and conservatively rejects whitespace followed by a digit, `-`, or `_` as a continuation.
- **R-18/R-19/R-20 — open-fulfiller griefing (medium/high).** One account is limited to one active open claim, but many accounts can claim in parallel. At zero bond, a claimant can hold an order for 15 minutes, and a submitted proof can move it into UnderReview beyond its 30-minute deadline. Buyer funds then need timeout/release/arbiter action. Suggested fix: nonzero slashable claim bond, cumulative per-order claim time, permissionless buyer refund after a final deadline, and a bounded arbitration service-level target.
- **R-26/R-27 — global caps and verification subsidy (high).** `max_orders/max_profiles` bound memory, yet a full cap is a whole-market denial of service. Each accepted proof uses a 0.01 NEAR OutLayer deposit from escrow; six attempts per order and the float check bound one order, not a Sybil fleet. Suggested fix: charge a refundable verification/storage fee or require a bond, cap total concurrent verifications, and alert on NEAR float, cap occupancy, and failed callbacks.
- **R-40/R-53/R-54/R-56 — operator trust (medium to critical).** The owner can upgrade code and change sources, the relayer can censor intents and directly submit proof, and the backend holds the only full spec/PII copy. In-flight callbacks are not tied to an immutable source revision. Generic hash/output checks prevent simple relayer spec tampering, but source trust and availability remain operational. Suggested fix: pin worker versions per order, use separate owner/arbiter keys with a timelock for upgrades, replicate encrypted spec storage, and provide an alternate relayer path.
- **Live zero-bond dispute recovery.** With immediate payout (`challenge_window_ns=0`) and `bond_per_job_bps=0`, a later buyer win after payout recovers **0 USDC** (`contracts/escrow/src/lib.rs:1265-1275`). This is an accepted hackathon limitation, not a refund guarantee. Raise a slashable bond before presenting post-payout disputes as buyer protection.

## 3. Live mainnet test procedure

The review wrote this procedure; the maintainers then ran it on mainnet (S1-S4, S6-S8; S5 skipped). Results and tx hashes: [MAINNET_ATTACK_TESTS.md](MAINNET_ATTACK_TESTS.md). Use only our `nearfullfil.near` deployment and the named test accounts. Choose one current catalog top-up or code product costing about 0.7–1 USDC. Stop/pause the official shopper process while preparing a negative-path order, so it cannot claim or buy before the test; restart it afterward. Reuse only **synthetic** phone/email values for tests that do not need real delivery. The examples use placeholders returned by the APIs; never put a real `cr_order_token` in a document or logs. Inspect the escrow receipt, `get_order`, `get_owed`, and intents balances after each action: a failed detached `auth_call` may leave the outer `execute_intents` successful. For every fund below, sign the returned `payload` with `kent-user1.near` and submit its signed form to `POST /api/v3/intents/submit`. A raw auth call intent has the shape `{"intent":"auth_call","contract_id":"nearfullfil.near","msg":"{\"op\":\"claim\",\"order_id\":\"FUL-…\"}","min_gas":"200000000000000"}`; sign it as the named account to `intents.near` and submit through the maintainers' driver. Every order costs its returned `quote.total` USDC while funded; a successful cancel/expire returns all USDC. Failed auth calls spend relayer gas only, around 0.001 NEAR each, not order USDC. An accepted proof attempt may consume up to the configured 0.01 NEAR OutLayer deposit from escrow.

1. **Non-quoter exclusive window.** `kent-user1.near`: `POST /api/v3/shop/quote` with `{"country":"VN","brand_name":"Viettel Mobile Credits","denomination":"20000 VND","beneficiary":"+84901234567","buyer_email":"synthetic@example.test"}`; fund using `payload`. Within two minutes, `fullfiller1.near` signs `{"op":"claim","order_id":"<quote.order_id>"}`. Expect escrow receipt panic **`quote exclusive`**, status `funded`, no USDC payout. Buyer signs `{"op":"cancel","order_id":"<id>"}`; order is pruned and all ~0.7–1 USDC returns. Demonstrates quote exclusivity even through raw intents.
2. **Code-kind allowlist.** Quote an in-stock code/eSIM product through the same API (use its exact catalog `brand_name` and `denomination`); verify `kind=cryptorefills_code`, then fund. `fullfiller1.near` attempts `claim`. Expect **`fulfiller not allowed`**, status `funded`. Buyer cancels and recovers the full quote total. Demonstrates the live allowlist; no product purchase is needed.
3. **Modified spec at submission.** Quote/fund a top-up, then `shopper.nearfullfil.near` claims. Shopper signs `{"op":"submit_proof","order_id":"<id>","proof_sha256":"2222222222222222222222222222222222222222222222222222222222222222","spec":"{}"}` with 200 TGas. Expect **`spec hash mismatch`** before OutLayer request or fee, status `claimed`. Shopper signs `release_claim`; buyer signs `cancel`; full USDC returns. Demonstrates buyer-signed spec binding against a malicious relayer/spec host.
4. **Cancel while claimed, then one refund.** Quote/fund a top-up; shopper claims. Buyer signs `{"op":"cancel","order_id":"<id>"}` and gets **`not cancellable`**; status remains `claimed` and escrow still holds the quote total. Shopper signs `release_claim`; buyer retries `cancel` and receives the full ~0.7–1 USDC. A third cancel gets **`order not found`**; intents buyer balance stays unchanged. Demonstrates refund/payout mutual exclusion and one-time refund.
5. **Old Cryptorefills purchase replay.** Quote/fund a top-up with the **same** product and beneficiary as the prior real E2E purchase. Shopper claims. The maintainers upload that prior purchase's ID/token through `POST /api/v3/proofs` body `{"order_id":"<id>","cr_order_id":"<old-id>","cr_order_token":"<runtime-secret>"}` and signs the returned `submit_proof` payload. Since the prior purchase is more than ten minutes older than this claim, expect status `proof_rejected`, `last_reject_reason="not ok: order_too_old"`, and no payout. The separate `is_nullifier_used(sha256("cryptorefills:<old-id>"))` view shows the prior ID is reserved; the age check runs first in this demo, so do **not** claim this run exercised the nullifier branch. Shopper releases, buyer cancels, full USDC returns; the attempt may cost escrow up to 0.01 NEAR.
6. **Wrong beneficiary proof.** Quote/fund the same top-up product but a **different** beneficiary from that prior purchase. Shopper claims; The maintainers upload the prior ID/token via the same proofs API and submits. Expect `proof_rejected` with **`not ok: beneficiary_mismatch`** (the worker checks beneficiary before age), no payout. Release and cancel for a full USDC refund; one verification attempt may cost up to 0.01 NEAR. Demonstrates the salted beneficiary binding even when a fulfiller knows a real order token.
7. **Level-0 double claim.** With shopper paused, quote/fund **two** top-ups, then wait until each order's 2-minute exclusive window ends. `fullfiller1.near` claims the first, then attempts `{"op":"claim","order_id":"<second-id>"}`. Check `get_profile(fullfiller1.near).level=0` before starting. Expect second receipt **`claim limit`**, first `claimed`, second `funded`. Fulfiller releases first; buyer cancels both, recovering both quote totals (~1.4–2 USDC). Demonstrates the per-account open-kind limit. If this fulfiller is level 1, use another registered level-0 test account instead.
8. **Release then deadline refund.** Quote/fund a top-up, shopper claims and signs `release_claim`. Leave it `funded` until its on-chain `deadline_ns` (about 30 minutes), then any account calls direct `expire({"order_id":"<id>"})`. Expect order pruned, full ~0.7–1 USDC returned to buyer; another `expire` fails **`order not found`**. This demonstrates permissionless refund after the deadline. Before the deadline, `expire` fails **`deadline not reached`**.

Run scenarios separately with unique order IDs, inspect each expected receipt before cleanup, and avoid any proof upload containing a live token except the two explicitly controlled replay cases. The old token is handled only at runtime. The first four and seventh scenarios require no merchant purchase; the two replay scenarios reuse a prior purchase and end in refund.
